Paper is a minimal, responsive, accessible and SEO-friendly [Cecil](https://cecil.app) blog theme, ported from [AstroPaper](https://github.com/satnaing/astro-paper). This theme follows best practices and provides accessibility out of the box. Light and dark mode are supported by default.

Read the blog posts or check the [README](https://github.com/Cecilapp/theme-paper#readme) for more info.
